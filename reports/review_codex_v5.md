### Dependency baseline, decoupling and statistical inference

- **The lane invariant holds at every tick.** Starting with empty lanes, zero intersections permit a new lane and one intersection permits expansion of that lane alone. Two or more intersections hold the PR in ready. Later entries intersecting the blocked cone union also wait and extend that union. Resolution only shrinks or removes active cones. Starts and completions have no other cone-expansion path. The argument covers equal-time arrivals and completions and concerns observed cones of live lane work. Latent edges can still cross lanes, and canceled speculative jobs are no longer live. The original bridge counterexample is now tested. (src/ssmq/simulator.py:352–367,624–701 and tests/test_ssmq.py:181–211.)
- **Fairness remains material.** A bridge may wait for all members of one or more coarse lanes to drain, including members irrelevant to its own dependency. Later PRs can also wait transitively behind the blocked cone union. The proxy does not speculate through this barrier via per-PR DAG prerequisites. Its scorer uses configured fault parameters and its recovery lacks a matched adaptive retry policy. SSMQ may run overlapping *separate batches* and use the oracle delta check at commit. Equal worker caps therefore leave unequal admission and recovery policies. DepTree's **132.3 min** P95 in the zero-conflict control already trails static FIFO's **36.7 min** substantially. The headline **79.0%** gap is evidence about this specific proxy. Report barrier time, lane widths, and retry sensitivity, and evaluate a more direct dependency comparator. (src/ssmq/simulator.py:415–470,506–701,732–748.) [SubmitQueue, EuroSys 2019](https://www.masoud.io/docs/eurosys19.pdf).
- **Exp5 exact density has a restricted range.** The generator fixes a target from a Bernoulli draw of on-graph pairs, retains round(ρ M) visible edges, and takes a weighted without-replacement prefix of disjoint pairs. All 60 matched blocks have identical counts at 60%, 80%, and 100%. All 20 TypeScript seeds lose edges at both 40% and 0%, while the other two repos stay matched. ## 4. Remaining strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths

- The bridge repair preserves a precisely stated invariant. The safety and scheduling propositions are intelligible under their pairwise and head-anchored models.
- Real Git and LOO verify that the intended intervention can execute. Strict nesting and ID counterbalancing make the sibling evidence interpretable within the program.
- The static comparison reports cost, rejections and throughput as well as latency. The uniform-defect result, conflict-free control and near-null factorial contrasts constrain the interpretation of the simulation.

### Submission blockers

1. **Workload provenance.** Reconcile the two Kubernetes omissions and distinguish the population of commit associations, merged PRs, and enqueue events. Text alone can accurately describe the currently sealed sample. Reasserting strict continuity requires repair and reanalysis.
2. **Independent evidence for graph efficacy.** The simulator constructs on-graph positives from the same cones used for scheduling and sees all persistent edges through an oracle. A risk-only control does not resolve the pair-label coupling. Independent conflict labels or materially narrower claims are needed.
3. **Attribution identified only on fixtures.** The Git comparison bundles packing and ranking and uses split accounting favorable to the named bisection metric. Exp8 finds negligible incremental effects. A matched live ranker ablation and independent fault cases are needed for the advertised practical contribution.
4. **Comparator validity and novelty.** The coarse lane barrier, retry asymmetry and untested stronger comparators materially weaken the outcome-tree headline. Prior conflict-aware grouping and predictive batching require direct discussion and comparison. EuroSys also requires evidence of concurrent exact-tree behavior and measured resource costs.
5. **Artifact delivery.** Provide review access instructions and verify the blinded distribution. Template and page count are resolved.

### Minor polish and camera-ready improvements

- Add the second vignette overlap and zero-based index convention. Repair the nondominated frontier caption and no-conflict sentinel. Use an exact-density label only on the matched range.
- Put absolute paired effects and acceptance alongside ratios. Report runner-minutes per merge and mean per-run survivor P95 consistently. Distinguish run invalidation, canceled computation, flake false rejection and persistent rejection.
- Tighten repeated abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusion language. Use separate Git and simulator escape macros and trace empirical literals to their independent sources.
