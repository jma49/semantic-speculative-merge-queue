# Batch 2 Baseline Coverage Sheet (`runs/v8_baselines`)

## Equal Experimental Protocol Invariants

- **Commit authority**: Strict exact-tree equality across every evaluated policy: a passing run may merge iff tested_tree == merged_mainline | members (zero commit-time oracle, zero escaped defects).
- **Capacity**: `workers=8`, `max_batch_size=8`, `max_spec_depth=4`, `ci_duration_min=12.0`, `ci_duration_std=2.0`.
- **Traces & arrivals**: Frozen v7 chronological merge traces (kubernetes/kubernetes, microsoft/TypeScript, vercel/next.js; 200 PRs each, 50 warm-up / 150 eval, zero omitted PRs, zero truncated files), rescaled to 6 PRs/hr baseline then velocity-multiplied (5x=30 PRs/hr nominal; 15x=90 PRs/hr saturated).
- **Priors & anti-leakage**: Decoupled fixed generic scheduler priors (q0=0.10, p_c=0.10, p_persist=0.02, Beta(1,19) flake prior) and warm-up-only scales/graphs; zero access to ground-truth rates or evaluation labels.
- **Uncertainty**: 20 paired seeds (1001..1020) x 3 repos = 60 paired blocks per condition; 10,000 paired seed-block bootstrap resamples (Random(11629)) retaining equal repo weight plus exact two-sided sign test.
- **No product speed claims**: Every product-inspired baseline models documented public scheduling/failure semantics under shared fixed-capacity exact-tree simulation; results are NOT benchmarks of commercial hosted services (GitHub, Aviator, Trunk, Uber SubmitQueue, BMW internal CI).
