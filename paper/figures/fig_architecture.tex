In evaluation, \texttt{ASTStackCausalRanker} (Algorithm~\ref{alg:blame}) is executed directly on 80 real Python \texttt{unittest} batches, and its measured Top-1/Top-2 accuracy and latency parameterize the discrete-event replay.}
