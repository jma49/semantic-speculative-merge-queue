\caption{End-to-end architecture of \textbf{SSMQ} under exact-tree commit authority. Warm-up traces build $\hat{G}$ and P90 risk scales with fixed generic scheduler priors. Pending retries, exact-tree stale rechecks ($M \neq M_0$), blame splits, and bisection sub-batches preempt fresh batches on $W$ parallel workers. When a remainder batch (\texttt{allow\_blame=False}) fails, it bypasses Step~5 and routes directly to bisection (Step~6). The simulator enforces identical exact-tree verification across all policies; the Git experiments execute real merges, test suites, and rankers.}
