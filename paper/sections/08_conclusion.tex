\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{SSMQ (Semantic Speculative Merge Queue)}, combining leak-free warm-up graph-disjoint risk-stratified packing, two-regime Beta-Binomial flaky gating, and fail-safe 1-step AST/traceback suspect ranking under a within-model cumulative safety check. Across \BenchTotalBatches{} executed failing Python \texttt{unittest} batches, \texttt{ASTStackCausalRanker} reaches \BenchASTTopOnePct{}\% Top-1 accuracy and \BenchASTLOOPct{}\% executed leave-one-out recovery in \BenchASTMs{}~ms mean (\BenchASTMaxMs{}~ms max), beating a same-information trivia-filtered file ranker by $75$ percentage points on functional sibling-function co-file edits (\BenchASTSiblingPct{}\% vs.\ \BenchFileTriviaSiblingPct{}\%). Across \TotalSealedRuns{} SHA-256-sealed suites (\TotalSealedRecords{} replays over 600 OSS PRs, \TotalEscapedDefects{} within-model cumulative escapes), SSMQ cuts P95 merge latency by \LatRedVsStaticPct{}\% vs.\ static FIFO batching ($p\HolmPLatVsStatic{}$) and \LatRedVsDepTreePct{}\% vs.\ dependency-aware outcome trees ($p\HolmPLatVsDepTree{}$), achieving the lowest CI runner cost among all parallel schedulers on each repository. Deconfounded ablations and fixed-density sweeps show that proactive cone-disjoint packing drives the primary speedup---persisting at $40\%$ recall and reversing at zero conflict---while asymmetric flaky gating bounds clean-PR rejections and keeps failed root batches small enough that 1-step blame chiefly benefits wide-batch culprit localization.
