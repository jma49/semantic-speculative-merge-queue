\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\subsection{Practical Takeaways}
Three operational lessons emerge for high-velocity queues: (1)~\textbf{Proactive cone-disjoint packing beats deep outcome trees} across our bursts, avoiding \DepTreeInvalidRatePct{}\% tree invalidations while preserving P95 gains down to $40\%$ recall and under uncorrelated uniform defects; (2)~\textbf{Asymmetric flaky gating} ($\tau_1=0.25$ vs.\ $\tau_B=0.50$) curbs clean-PR false rejections for $\phi > 0\%$ without fixed-retry tail inflation (whereas no-retry is slightly faster at $\phi=0\%$); and (3)~\textbf{Static AST/traceback ranking} runs in \BenchASTMs{}~ms and cuts real bisection splits by \GitReplaySplitSavePct{}\% on wider failing batches (RQ1), whereas proactive packing keeps macro failed roots near \FacEightKEightFailSz{}~PRs (RQ5).

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\textbf{Construct Validity.} Our 600-PR corpus records consecutive default-branch merges, but $5$~PRs/repo exceed GitHub's 100-file GraphQL page cap, and merge timestamps act as departure-derived proxies rather than enqueue logs with rejected attempts. Our macro simulator parameterizes defect rates, conflict density, constant-mean CI duration, independent flakes, and RQ1 blame accuracy rather than rebasing live repositories; we mitigate this via our 10-seed real Git-tree replay running live \texttt{git merge}, \texttt{unittest}, and \texttt{ASTStackCausalRanker} with no synthetic oracle, plus $r_{\text{corr}}=0$ and $0\%$-recall controls. Our Python benchmark targets synchronous \texttt{FunctionDef} edits and bare-name calls on one six-module codebase; polyglot monorepos with \texttt{async} dispatch, reflection, or same-function competing edits need language-specific parsers (e.g., Tree-sitter) and rely on bisection fallback when Top-1 misses.

\textbf{Internal and External Validity.} Paired $(\text{repo}, \text{seed})$ draws, leak-free warm-up priors, exact density matching across $\rho \in [60\%, 100\%]$, Holm-Bonferroni correction, and A/A checks bound stochastic variance, though inferences remain conditional on three OSS traces and finite 150-PR burst drains rather than stationary infinite-horizon queues. Cached heterogeneous builds or correlated flake bursts~\cite{parry2025systemic} may require retuning $K_{\max}$, $\theta_{\text{risk}}$, and $(\tau_1, \tau_B)$. Shadow deployment on live monorepo CI is our primary next step.
