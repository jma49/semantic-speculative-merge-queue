\section{Discussion, Threats to Validity, and Artifact}
\label{sec:threats}

\subsection{Practical Takeaways}
Three engineering lessons emerge for high-velocity merge queues:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Proactive Cone-Disjoint Packing Beats Deep Speculation}: Even dependency-aware outcome trees (\texttt{DepAwareOutcomeTree}) suffer \DepTreeInvalidRatePct{}\% speculative invalidations under bursty load, whereas warm-up co-change and two-level directory packing requires no build instrumentation and retains P95 gains down to $40\%$ graph recall.
    \item \textbf{Separate Singleton and Batch Flaky Thresholds}: Judging queues solely by merged-PR latency hides clean-PR rejections (\FlakyFifteenNoFlakyFRPct{}\% on \texttt{SSMQ-NoFlaky} at $\phi=15\%$). Guarding singletons ($\tau_1 = 0.25$) while splitting failing batches ($\tau_B = 0.50$) avoids both false rejections and fixed-retry tail inflation.
    \item \textbf{Deploy Static AST Blame for Wide Batches and Triage}: Because cone-disjoint packing holds failed root batches near \FacEightKEightFailSz{}~PRs, 1-step blame barely shifts end-to-end latency inside SSMQ; its primary value is fast (\BenchASTMs{}~ms) localization on wide batches and developer triage where file overlap fails on sibling functions (\BenchASTSiblingPct{}\% vs.\ \BenchFileTriviaSiblingPct{}\%).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\textbf{Construct Validity.} Our 600-PR traces capture real file topologies and hotspot skew, but GitHub GraphQL caps PRs at $40$ paths (truncating $6$, $8$, and $16$ PRs across traces), trace windows span $20$~days to $7.6$~yr, and merge timestamps serve as departure-derived arrival proxies rather than enqueue logs. Our replay models pairwise conflicts and persistent checks rather than rebasing Git trees on live CI runners, parameterizing defect rates, conflict density, constant-mean CI duration, independent flakes, and RQ1 blame accuracy rather than running live tracebacks inside the queue loop. Our Python benchmark targets synchronous \texttt{FunctionDef} AST edits and bare-name calls on preloaded strings; polyglot monorepos with \texttt{async} dispatch, reflection, or compiled crash dumps require language-specific parsers (e.g., Tree-sitter) and may yield lower Top-1 accuracy, which Algorithm~\ref{alg:flaky} absorbs via bisection fallback.

\textbf{Internal and Conclusion Validity.} Paired $(\text{repo}, \text{seed})$ fault draws, leak-free warm-up priors, fixed-density recall controls, Holm-Bonferroni adjustments, and A/A noise checks bound experimental drift; however, all inferences remain conditional on the three OSS traces and finite 150-PR burst drains rather than stationary infinite-horizon queues or random repository samples.

\textbf{External Validity.} Environments with cached heterogeneous build times, correlated flake storms~\cite{parry2025systemic}, or non-Python stacks may require retuning $K_{\max}$, $\theta_{\text{risk}}$, and $(\tau_1, \tau_B)$. Shadow-mode validation atop live Git rebases is our immediate next step.
